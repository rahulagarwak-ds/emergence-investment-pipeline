## Key risks
- Entrenched incumbents with proven deployments and OEM/dealership integrations could lengthen sales cycles and hinder displacement. [E5](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/uveye-and-carfax-launch-integration-to-improve-vehicle-appraisals-and-damage-detection-for-dealerships-302807489.html>) [E6](<https://www.coxautoinc.com/press-releases/cox-automotives-vauto-and-uveye-bring-ai-powered-vehicle-inspections-to-market/>)
- Customers may resist switching from existing app-based/manual workflows, requiring integrations and change management. [E7](<https://www.ravin.ai/>) [E8](<https://www.damageid.com/product/>)
- Very early stage with limited reference customers, as indicated by current partner onboarding status. [E3 · self-reported](<https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dream>)

## What would change the decision
- What accuracy, recall, and precision does the system achieve on dent/scrape detection across vehicle types and conditions?
- Do they have pilots or paying customers with quantified outcomes (caught-damage rate, dispute reduction, incremental revenue)?
- What is the pricing model and expected ROI/payback versus missed-damage recovery?
