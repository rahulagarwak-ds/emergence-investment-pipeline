## Key risks
- Marker’s terms exclude use where regulations such as HIPAA and FISMA apply, creating a potential enterprise security and compliance blocker. [E11 · self-reported](<https://onmarker.com/terms/>)
- The embedded, custom-build delivery model may be labor-intensive, and its scalability is not established by the supplied evidence. [E4 · self-reported](<https://onmarker.com/>) [E7 · self-reported](<https://onmarker.com/terms/>)
- Reported customer outcomes lack named customers, independent validation, baseline definitions, and financial impact data. [E6 · self-reported](<https://onmarker.com/>)

## What would change the decision
- Can Marker demonstrate a repeatable procurement path, pricing model, and expansion from an initial workflow to broader team adoption?
- Can the company provide independent customer references and validate the reported operational outcomes?
- Can Marker provide security controls, deployment options, and compliance coverage sufficient for regulated enterprise use?
