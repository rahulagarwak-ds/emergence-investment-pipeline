## Key risks
- Execution depends on reliable compliance across Medicaid eligibility, enrollment, EVV, payroll, and payer billing. [E3 · self-reported](<https://cova.care/>) [E5 · self-reported](<https://cova.care/resources>)
- The model is concentrated in Michigan, leaving portability across states and payer systems unproven. [E1 · self-reported](<https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cova>) [E3 · self-reported](<https://cova.care/>)
- The AI-native back office is central to the operating model, making agent reliability, oversight, and compliance material risks. [E1 · self-reported](<https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cova>)

## What would change the decision
- What current enrollment, patient, caregiver, revenue, margin, and retention metrics would demonstrate meaningful traction?
- Which Michigan payers currently contract with Cova, and what evidence supports expansion beyond Michigan?
- What controls, audits, and human oversight ensure the AI-enabled workflow is reliable and compliant?
