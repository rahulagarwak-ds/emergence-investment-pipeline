## Key risks
- The model depends on local staffing, property access, cleaners, vendors, and market-specific regulatory compliance, with serviceability varying by property. [E3 · self-reported](<https://www.atlia.com/short-term-rental-property-management/>) [E5 · self-reported](<https://www.atlia.com/airbnb-property-management-san-diego/>)
- The stated 10% fee excludes several owner costs, including cleaning, repairs, supplies, platform fees, taxes, permits, and insurance, which may pressure customer economics. [E4 · self-reported](<https://www.atlia.com/about/>) [E6 · self-reported](<https://www.atlia.com/airbnb-property-management-cost/>)
- The company does not guarantee revenue, occupancy, profit, or regulatory eligibility, leaving performance improvement and scalability unverified. [E5 · self-reported](<https://www.atlia.com/airbnb-property-management-san-diego/>)

## What would change the decision
- What are the current property count, revenue, retention, occupancy, owner payback, and contribution margins?
- How much of the workflow is automated versus performed by local operators, and how do automation and staffing ratios improve unit economics with scale?
- What is the geographic expansion model, including local staffing, vendor coverage, and market-by-market regulatory operations?
