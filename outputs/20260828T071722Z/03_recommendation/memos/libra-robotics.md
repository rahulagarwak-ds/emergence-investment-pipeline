[site_safety · self-reported](<https://www.librabots.com/safety/>) [site_home · self-reported](<https://www.librabots.com/>) [site_solutions · self-reported](<https://www.librabots.com/solutions/solar-installation/>)

## What would change the decision
- What are the detailed RaaS unit economics (pricing per MW/module, mobilization fees, margins), and how do they compare to incumbent methods?
- How many paying customers and production (non-pilot) contracts exist today, and what revenue has been realized?
- What is verified field productivity across racking systems and site conditions versus the published design targets?
