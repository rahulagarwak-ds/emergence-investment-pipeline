## What would change the decision
- Are the 99.9%+ accuracy and ROI claims independently validated by customers or third parties?
- Which of the 30+ integrations are GA and bi-directional today, and how deep is the workflow coverage?
- What deployment models and data residency options are available (e.g., VPC or on-prem) for regulated buyers?
